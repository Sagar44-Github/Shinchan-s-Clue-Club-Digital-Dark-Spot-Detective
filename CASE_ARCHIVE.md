# Shinchan's Mystery Files

The archive contains eight ready-to-open training investigations. Opening a file creates a separate case in the local case room, copies in its complete source pack and checklist, and runs the evidence patrol. It does not change the library template.

Every organization, place, schedule, price, email, phone number, and policy in these cases is fictional. Source titles are marked as samples and source text begins with a sample-only notice. The examples teach comparison and verification; they are not real-world instructions.

| Investigation | Source pack | Investigation work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Shinchan's Scholarship Puzzle** | Current round notice, archived FAQ, department checklist | Reconcile round-specific deadline and timezone, compare document lists, distinguish a current notice from an old cycle, and identify missing renewal criteria. |
| **Shiro's Rainy-Day Rescue** | Ward bulletin, shelter access sheet, roadworks update | Compare drill times and approach routes, inspect step-free access claims against construction dates, and identify missing detour confirmation. |
| **Crayon Carnival Access Mystery** | Visitor page, park map, organizer update | Trace a changed step-free entrance, resolve quiet-room location and hours, and separate gate opening from festival admission. |
| **Nohara's Moving-Day Money Mystery** | Public listing, fee schedule, unsigned lease summary | Compare recurring rent with one-time fees, investigate conflicting deposit/key-fee terms, and find missing refund and cancellation detail. |
| **Shinchan's Night Bus Mission** | Autumn timetable, roadworks alert, stop access register | Track an active detour, inspect whether the last trip changes, and distinguish low-floor vehicle claims from stop-level access. |
| **Shiro's Gadget Recycling Hunt** | Community flyer, accepted-items register, operations note | Compare collection hours, item limits, batteries, data preparation, and the later handling fee for one item class. |
| **The Nohara Neighborhood Garden Quest** | Current grant notice, eligibility guide, prior-cycle worksheet | Compare applicant eligibility and attachments, separate current-round details from an old worksheet, and find unconfirmed match and reporting rules. |
| **The Sticky Subscription Mystery** | Product page, cancellation help article, billing terms | Reconstruct the renewal timestamp, cutoff timezone, refund language, trial deadline, and post-cancellation data export window. |

## How the archive exercises the product

- **Source Locker:** inspect complete source text, publisher, stated date, capture time, and content hash.
- **Case Timeline:** put publication, retrieval, patrol, and verified-task events in sequence.
- **3D Evidence Map:** explore Shinchan's clubhouse detective desk, select source folders and finding markers, and open their evidence.
- **Claim Workshop:** filter extracted passages by the checklist topic and open the source record behind each claim.
- **Conflict Lab:** review detected comparison signals and choose any two records for a direct text comparison.
- **Requirement Studio:** add or remove standard checks, create a case-specific requirement, and rerun the patrol after editing.
- **Mission Board:** work through the evidence-linked follow-up tasks and record what was confirmed.
- **Case Briefing:** export the current analysis as a source-linked Markdown file.

Sample contacts use the reserved `.invalid` domain or fictional `555` numbers. This is intentional: the sample archive cannot be mistaken for an official source.
